# ORBIT Privacy Notice

Last updated: 10 November 2026

ORBIT is an independent desktop application. This notice describes data handling visible in the current public desktop source code. The application does not require an ORBIT account.

## Information stored on your device

- Radio and music favourites, saved Spotify item links, and related interface preferences are stored locally in the application's browser storage.
- Offline media files and their library metadata are stored in the application's local user-data folder on your device.
- When you connect Spotify, ORBIT uses Spotify's authorization flow. Spotify access and refresh tokens are held in the application's session storage to support the connection; they are not an ORBIT account password.

You can remove saved items through the application's controls. You can remove offline media through ORBIT's downloads controls. Uninstalling ORBIT may not remove all user data; use Windows or the application's data controls to remove it.

## Network services

ORBIT contacts third-party services when you use features that depend on them. Depending on the feature, requests may include search terms, station or media identifiers, playback requests, and ordinary network information such as your IP address. Services visible in the current source include Spinn Radio, Radio Browser, Spotify, YouTube, Audius, Jamendo, Apple/iTunes catalogues, LibriVox, and Internet Archive. Some media is embedded or played directly by those providers.

When you choose to connect Spotify, Spotify receives the authorization request and processes the permissions you approve. ORBIT requests the Spotify scopes shown in its connection flow. You can revoke ORBIT's access from your Spotify account settings.

These providers operate independently of ORBIT and process information under their own privacy terms. ORBIT does not control their data retention, cookies, analytics, or processing practices. Please review the relevant provider privacy policies before using their services.

## Analytics and advertising

The reviewed desktop source does not include a first-party analytics or advertising SDK. Third-party websites or embedded services may apply their own cookies, analytics, or advertising policies when you use them.

## Sharing and sale

ORBIT does not operate an ORBIT user-account backend and does not sell a user profile or locally stored favourites/downloads. Information sent directly to a third-party provider is governed by that provider's privacy policy and the feature you choose to use.

## Security

ORBIT stores favourites and offline media locally. Spotify authorization tokens are kept in session storage. Do not share your device account or local application data with people you do not trust. No software can guarantee absolute security.

## Changes and contact

This notice may be updated as the application and its third-party integrations change. To ask a privacy question or report a concern, open an issue at [ORBIT Desktop on GitHub](https://github.com/Nin01234/ORBIT-Desktop/issues).
